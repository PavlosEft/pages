Mediation: A substantive solution, without unnecessary conflict

Mediation offers a modern, fast, and effective way to resolve disputes, avoiding time-consuming court confrontations. It gives the parties themselves control over the solution, with the help of a neutral and specialized mediator.

It promotes dialogue, protects relationships, and allows for solutions tailored to the real needs of each case. At the same time, it can significantly reduce the costs, mental burden, and uncertainty of litigation.

Because the best resolution to a dispute is not always winning the trial — but winning the solution.

Legal knowledge with consistency and a personal approach

I am a lawyer, a graduate of the Law School of Komotini, holding two master's degrees and possessing particular experience in the fields of enforcement law and the National Cadastre. During my traineeship, I actively handled Cadastre cases, representing financial institutions and funds, gaining valuable experience in managing demanding and complex cases.

Today, as a new lawyer, I have chosen to focus on active litigation, approaching each case with responsibility, perseverance, and a commitment to substantive client representation. Although I am at the beginning of my professional career, I believe that continuous development and personal involvement in every case are the elements that make a difference.

I attach particular importance to direct and meaningful communication with the client, ensuring clear information, an understanding of available options, and a personalized legal approach.

My goal is to provide reliable legal support, with a personal touch, consistency, and dedication to the needs of each client.
